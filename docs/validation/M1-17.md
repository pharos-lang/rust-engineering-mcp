# M1-17 — 0.1.0 qualification

Status: **In progress**. ADR-048 resolved the host, artifact and catalog scope, but
M1 and 0.1.0 are not closed or distributed as binary releases. The source repository
is public under ADR-047. Closure now requires executable evidence on the final
candidate rather than another owner decision.

Completed evidence:

- the release binary is source-equivalent across all 238 selected inputs to
  candidate commit `d024c7c`;
- the fresh 19-stage macOS ARM64 gate passed; its preserved log derives 644
  workspace-test passes plus one separate doctest, and its focused audit repeat
  records the exact dirty local RustSec input identity;
- MCP Inspector 2.5.0 discovered and successfully called all thirteen tools through
  its persistent UI; its Resource-read and cancellation limits remain explicit;
- stock Codex 0.153.0 direct client APIs passed tool calls, Resource read, stable
  canonical inventory, E0502-to-green repair mechanics and a clear missing-runtime
  denial; two model-driven attempts failed without a product call and are retained;
- the final stock Codex 0.153.0 model-directed run used the exact core binary,
  observed E0502, made one source-only correction, rechecked green and separately
  observed the exact missing-runtime denial under a hash-bound closed profile;
- the final Inspector 2.5.0 repetition discovered all thirteen tools and exercised
  bounded positive and fail-closed paths on the same core binary;
- the bounded M1-16 pilot completed 24/24 runs, but its saturated endpoint has zero
  discriminating power and B consumed more observed requests, tokens and time;
- the separate native retrieval benchmark completed one bounded 8-query/15-crate
  descriptive run, without a general quality, multilingual or agent-utility claim;
- the read-only Opus 5 final review is preserved and every finding has a principal
  disposition.
- the final 23-stage full gate v2 passed on commit `d24a95d` with native start/end
  timestamps, direct test counts and empty post-gate managed Docker inventory;

The authoritative requirement, §117 and native-platform mappings are in the
[qualification matrix](M1-17-matrix.md). The [review disposition](M1-17-review-disposition.md)
preserves the historical findings. ADR-048 supersedes their release-blocker
disposition without rewriting the original review:

1. 0.1.0 publishes only a core `aarch64-apple-darwin` archive; Linux/Windows remain
   portable/fail-closed CI and receive no artifacts;
2. the full `local` profile remains source-qualified on macOS26 ARM64/APFS with the
   Docker Linux ARM64 guest, but model/ORT/LanceDB bytes are not redistributed;
3. no official catalog or production Ed25519 key exists for 0.1.0.

M1-17 remains In progress pending final candidate review/disposition, public PR/CI,
tag, verified attestation and published release over the same candidate bytes.

No M2 work was started. The historical qualification itself performed no remote
operation; ADR-047 later authorized the separate public source snapshot.
